About

Meet the Person
Behind the Bridge

I'm Deborah Akua Abbey, a mathematics teacher, Upper Secondary Coordinator, and the founder of The Math Bridge.

I hold a BSc in Mathematics from KNUST and a PGDE from the University of Education, Winneba. I currently teach Cambridge IGCSE and IB MYP Mathematics at Brilliant Maths and Science Education Centre in Accra, where I work with students across junior and senior secondary levels.

The Math Bridge was born out of a simple conviction: mathematics is not difficult it is just too often taught without context. Every edition, every tutoring session, every resource I create is built on one mission: to make mathematics feel less like a barrier and more like a bridge.

We use market calculations, farmland problems, and everyday Ghanaian life to make abstract mathematics tangible so students see mathematics not as a foreign language but as a tool they already use.
