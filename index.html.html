About

Your Guide to Easy Math Understanding

The Math Bridge is an independent educational brand built on one belief: every student can understand mathematics when it's taught with clarity, care, and cultural relevance. Founded by Deborah Akua Abbey — a mathematics teacher, facilitator, and curriculum expert — the platform uses narrative storytelling and West African contexts to make abstract concepts feel real.

Through a weekly newsletter, tutoring materials, and community resources, The Math Bridge supports students preparing for Cambridge IGCSE and IB MYP examinations, as well as parents and educators looking to build stronger mathematical foundations in their communities.

Our approach is simple: understand first, memorize second. We use market calculations, farmland problems, and everyday Ghanaian life to make abstract mathematics tangible — so students see mathematics not as a foreign language but as a tool they already use.
